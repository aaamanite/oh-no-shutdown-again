Shift briefing

You are the new OT Security Lead at PMU Bukit Mutiara, the 132/33 kV substation at the heart of the ONSA Power & Co. grid. A biogas power plant, an offshore wind farm and a solar farm feed it. It supplies 12,400 homes, Hospital Bukit Mutiara, Kolej Komuniti Bukit Mutiara, Mutiara Avenue Mall, ONSA's HQ and the R&D Centre, where researchers are preparing the next big project: space-based solar power, beamed down wirelessly. Attackers have noticed — and nobody wants to hear “shutdown” again.

  1. Keep the lights on. Every energized feeder earns revenue. Outages cost money, reliability and regulator fines.
  2. Invest in controls. Asset inventory, monitoring, segmentation, MFA, backups — each one prevents or exposes real attack techniques.
  3. Triage incidents. Read what each system logged, mark each line True Positive or False Positive, then respond or remediate. Overreacting is a failure too.
